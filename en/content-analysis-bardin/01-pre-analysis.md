# Bardin: Pre-Analysis of Content

Skill to operationalize and rigorously document Phase 1 (Pre-Analysis) of Laurence Bardin's Content Analysis methodology (2011/2016). Ensures that selection, organization, and preparation of the textual corpus follow auditable parameters for academic examination boards.

## When to Use

- In the initial stage of qualitative research (theses, dissertations, capstone projects, and papers) using Bardin's Content Analysis.
- To structure the floating reading journal and organize preliminary impressions of the material.
- To audit and methodologically justify the constitution of the analytical corpus.
- To formalize objectives, hypotheses (reading guidelines), and indicators/indices prior to coding.

## Steps of Pre-Analysis

### 1. Conducting Floating Reading
- Establish direct and unstructured contact with raw documents (interviews, news, institutional documents, surveys).
- Record initial impressions, emerging hypotheses, and prominent themes without imposing premature categorization.
- Build the Floating Reading Matrix recording: Document ID, Highlighted Excerpts, Initial Impression, and Relevant Question.

### 2. Auditing and Establishing the Corpus (Bardin's 4 Rules)
Evaluate the document set against Bardin's four formal criteria:
1. **Rule of Exhaustiveness:** Confirm that all delimited documents were included without arbitrary or convenient omissions.
2. **Rule of Representativeness:** Verify whether the sample (when applicable) accurately represents the source universe.
3. **Rule of Homogeneity:** Ensure documents were obtained through identical and comparable procedures and criteria.
4. **Rule of Relevance:** Validate that document content aligns with research objectives and questions.

### 3. Formalizing Objectives, Hypotheses, and Indices
- **Hypotheses and Reading Questions:** Formulate theoretical guidelines that will direct textual search (analytical directions for exploration).
- **Indicators and Indices:** Define which formal elements (keywords, sentence structures, themes) will be tracked during the exploration phase.

### 4. Generating the Corpus Protocol
Produce a synthesis document containing:
- Detailed description of the sample/corpus.
- Validation grid for the 4 rules with methodological justification.
- Roadmap of hypotheses and indices for Phase 2 (Coding).

## Gotchas

- **Premature Categorization:** Do not attempt to create final categories during floating reading. Respect the methodological latency time.
- **Vagueness in Defense:** Avoid justifying material selection with generic phrases like "exhaustive reading". Use the auditing grid of Bardin's 4 rules as documentary evidence.
- **Lack of Inclusion/Exclusion Criteria:** If a document is disregarded, record the exact reason based on the rule of homogeneity or relevance.
